Languages and imports

The app is available in German and English. CSV import currently supports the DKB format.

We are happy to add further languages and CSV formats from other banks on request. Email info@thomasmenzl.de — for a new import, please attach an anonymized sample file.

App Store

  • Free tier for daily use — data stays on your device.
  • Optional Pro as a one-time purchase (power features) — no subscription for the core app, no ads.
  • Optional iCloud Sync as a yearly subscription, independent of Pro. Opt-in under Settings → iCloud Sync; data stays in your private iCloud, no first-party server.
  • Restore purchases: in the app under Settings → License.

About the app
