Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Controller: Thomas Menzl Softwareentwicklung

1. Overview

Finanzübersicht is a personal finance app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. The app includes no third-party tracking, ads, or analytics SDKs.

2. On-device data

All finance and settings data are stored locally on your device, including:

  • Accounts, balances, transactions, recurring payments
  • Categories, budgets, and savings goals
  • App preferences (language, appearance)
  • optional local backups / exports you create yourself
  • Status of optional unlocks (in-app purchases)

This data does not leave your device via our servers — we do not operate a backend for your finance data. Cloud sync is not part of the app today.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases (e.g. Pro) are processed by Apple (App Store / StoreKit). Payment and account data are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive full payment details.

4. No sharing

We do not sell or share personal usage data with advertising networks or data brokers.

5. App Store / platform

If you get the app via the Apple App Store, Apple may process purchase, download, and device information under Apple’s privacy policy.
